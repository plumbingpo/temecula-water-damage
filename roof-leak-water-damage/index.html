Dealing With Roof Leak Water Damage in Temecula

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. A ceiling stain tends to arrive late, well after the space overhead first got wet. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

Our trade is water damage restoration, and roofing is a separate one. Our job is the inside of the house: pulling out soaked insulation, drying the framing and drywall, treating for mold and putting the ceiling back together. When the roof is still letting water through, a temporary tarp is something we can install, and we will coordinate with your roofer so the repair comes before the ceiling is closed.

First Five Minutes

Before We Arrive

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesTurn off the breaker for any light, fan or outlet that has water running through or near it, then keep your hands off it.
  2. Catch the DripsPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Protect the floor with plastic sheeting or old towels.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Document What You SeeTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.
Insurance

Insurance and Roof Leak Water Damage

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Why It Happens

How It Usually Starts

  • A valley liner that has worn through at the point where the most runoff passes.
  • Fasteners working loose over time, so water follows the nail shank into the attic.
  • Clogged gutters that back water up under the shingles at the roof edge.
  • A vent pipe boot whose rubber has split from long sun exposure.
  • Skylight seals and curbs that dry out, crack and let rain run inside.

What the Water Damages

Attic Insulation

Once soaked, insulation flattens, loses its value and keeps the drywall under it damp for days. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Cellulose that is saturated generally comes out.

Drywall Ceilings

Drywall that stays wet stains, sags and loses its strength. Joints open, tape peels, and a waterlogged piece may fall without much notice.

Mold in the Attic

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. Growth can begin within 24 to 48 hours and spread across the underside of the decking.

Wall Cavities

Water that reaches the top of a wall runs down inside it, wetting insulation and the back of the drywall. Bubbling paint or a swollen baseboard may be the only sign.

How We Handle Roof Leak Water Damage

1

Trace the Leak Path

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters, our team checks wall cavities, ceilings and the attic and charts the whole route the water took, even where the room below shows nothing.

2

Tarp and Protect

If the roof is still open to the weather, we can install a temporary tarp or coordinate with your roofer. We also cover floors and contents under the damaged ceiling.

3

Tear Out and Dry

After removing the insulation and drywall that are past saving, our crew dries the attic and lower rooms with air movers and dehumidifiers and tracks moisture levels until the wood is dry.

4

Treat and Rebuild

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. Once the roof repair is done, we replace insulation, hang and finish new drywall, then prime and paint to match the ceiling.
