Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it

How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward

Why Storm Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

Storm damage creates complex, connected repair needs that take experienced restoration professionals to handle properly. A roof leak that looks minor from inside may have done extensive hidden damage to insulation, roof decking and wall cavities, and that leads to mold growth and structural deterioration if it is not found and dried. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging and moisture meters to trace water paths and identify every affected area.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. An improperly installed tarp can create new problems by channeling water or failing under wind load. Experienced in storm mitigation, our team picks appropriate methods and materials to keep the building protected until permanent repair work can be scheduled.
