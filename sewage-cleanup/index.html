A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Temecula Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Temecula, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Temecula

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
